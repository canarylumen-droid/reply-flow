## Strategy 3: Buying-Signal & Intent-Triggered Prospecting

Rather than reaching out to companies randomly, high-performing B2B teams only contact accounts exhibiting **active buying signals**:

| Buying Signal | What It Means | Recommended Action |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Hiring Surge in Key Role** | Company is expanding that department and feeling pain | Reach out with an operational efficiency offer |
| **New Executive Leadership** | New VP or CMO looking to make an impact in first 90 days | Offer a strategic audit or modernization plan |
| **Technology Change** | Installed or dropped a competitor tool | Target with a migration or replacement campaign |
| **High Intent Content Consumption** | Reading comparison articles or pricing pages | Trigger an automated conversational outreach |

When you reach out to a company at the exact moment their pain is acute, your outbound response rates jump from 2% to over 15%.
